# ETF Theme Research

## Define the theme

1. State the theme in one sentence without naming a security recommendation.
2. List inclusion terms, aliases, adjacent concepts, and explicit exclusions.
3. State the economic mechanism as a hypothesis, not as a fact.
4. Form questions that could confirm or disconfirm the hypothesis.
5. Present the definition for human confirmation before research begins.

## Build the evidence case

- Prefer official filings, company disclosures, academic records, patents, official job boards, and issuer holdings.
- Treat search engines, public discussions, and browser discoveries as leads until corroborated.
- Preserve the evidence ID, original URL, publisher, date, source tier, content hash, and limitation.
- Seek independent publishers and source types instead of repeating the same announcement.
- Search explicitly for counter-evidence, missing commercial adoption, concentration, competing explanations, and stale signals.

## Write the report

- Cite only evidence IDs and URLs present in the supplied evidence package.
- Produce an evidence-bound Chinese title, fact summary, and research implication for every cited supporting and counter item. Preserve official brand, company, ticker, and protocol names.
- Reject translations that omit or duplicate an evidence ID, introduce a new number, company, URL, or fact, or change the source's certainty.
- Separate verified facts, research implications, assumptions, unknowns, and disconfirming conditions.
- Never invent numbers, companies, ETFs, holdings, historical trends, or product white space.
- Mark unavailable investability, historical, or overlap data as `unknown` or `insufficient_data`.
- Keep WATCH, DEEP_RESEARCH, REJECT, scoring, and approval decisions under deterministic application control.
- Do not generate, infer, or complete ETF market figures. Market snapshots belong to the deterministic Yahoo/yfinance tool and remain independent from immutable report versions.
- End with evidence gaps and concrete next research actions; do not provide investment, trading, portfolio, legal, tax, or ETF issuance advice.
